## 2. Engine #1 — Base Expansion (this IS the $4M engine)

### Step 1 — Score all 75 now (this week). Zero onboarding.
You **operate their infrastructure already** — so you don't need them to connect anything. Run the Governance Score across the base using data you control. This is your unfair, instant advantage: **the base has zero onboarding friction.** Output per client: 0–100 score, per-domain breakdown (infra/cyber/AI/data), top gaps, peer-benchmark position.

### Step 2 — Rank by conversion value.
Sort by **gap severity × regulatory exposure × account size**. This ranking *is* your outreach order. High-gap + high-exposure + large = call first. The Score does the qualification so you don't have to.

### Step 3 — The "Governance Readiness Review" (the concierge motion = your awareness answer).
Awareness for the base is **not ads** — it's a personal, founder-led review. The Score is the artifact that manufactures urgency ("here's your exam exposure, here's where you rank vs. peers"). Sequence:
1. **Personal note** (you → client): *"We ran a governance/exam-readiness snapshot on [Firm]. Two gaps stood out. 20 minutes to walk you through it?"* — warm, specific, no pitch.
2. **The Review (30–45 min):** present score → the 2–3 gaps that map to real exam findings → what "continuous + evidenced" looks like → the offer (Platform bundle or Managed).
3. **Proposal within 24h** (templated, see §4).
4. **Close** — bundle Platform onto the existing MSP invoice (lowest-friction yes: one vendor, one bill, one relationship).

### Step 4 — Whale track (proof + revenue + referrals in one).
Your few whales already onboard are worth more as **proof engine** than as revenue alone:
- Convert 3–5 to **Managed** (big ACV).
- Turn each into a **case study** (anonymized if needed) + a **benchmark data point**.
- **Ask every whale for 2 warm intros** to peer firms (custodian networks, industry groups). Warm referral is the only "new logo" channel that closes in H2.

## 3. Replicating your knowledge so it isn't all you (the scale answer)

You can't personally run 40+ reviews *and* build the company. Here's how the expertise gets out of your head — **the Score + templates do the diagnosis, recordings become the training corpus, the app drafts the artifacts.**

### The knowledge-capture flywheel
1. **The Score removes the need for an expert to find gaps** — a trained account manager (even an existing Compuwork one) can run a review because the diagnosis is already done.
2. **Record your first ~10 founder-led reviews** (Granola is connected — capture every one).
3. **Transcribe → extract** the winning narrative, the objections you heard, and your answers → build the enablement kit:
   - **Governance Review script** (talk track, slide-by-slide).
   - **Objection library** (real objections + your real answers).
   - **Proposal + pricing templates** (Platform bundle / Managed).
   - **Gap-report + WSP drafts** — generated by the app (Claude), not written by hand.
4. **Package as "Governance Review in a Box"** → hand to the first hire.
5. **The app embeds the expertise** — WSP generator, gap reports, exam packages mean a junior person delivers senior-quality artifacts.

### What to systematize *before* you hire (so training is days, not months)
- [ ] Score → ranked account list (repeatable, from the app).
- [ ] Review script + deck + recording library (from your first 10).
- [ ] Proposal/pricing templates + approval rules.
- [ ] Objection library + FAQ.
- [ ] A 2-week "governance activation" onboarding runbook (below).

## 5. Sales plan (the operating system)

**Two pipelines in HubSpot** — *Platform* (base Core + warm SMB) and *Managed* (whales + larger warm). Shared stages:

`Scored → Review booked → Review held → Proposal sent → Verbal → Closed-won`

**Founder cadence (H2):**
- Target **6–8 reviews/week** (adjust to your real availability — see questions).
- Proposals out within 24h of every review.
- Weekly pipeline review (you + Claude): conversion by stage, ARR booked vs. the month's target, next-week review list.

**Weekly KPIs:** reviews held · proposals sent · close rate · ARR booked · base % scored · base % converted · whale referrals collected.

**First-hire trigger:** when your active pipeline exceeds ~15 open reviews **or** after 10 recorded reviews (playbook ready) — whichever first. First hire = **Governance Account Manager** (runs Core reviews from the playbook). Second = **marketing/GTM ops generalist** (runs the funnel + HubSpot/Make). Don't hire before the system exists — you'll spend the time training instead of selling.
