# Centience — LinkedIn Outreach Sequence (July 2026 list)

A multi-touch LinkedIn cadence for the 316-lead July 2026 list. Built for a
regulated, skeptical audience: peer-level tone, insight first, one soft CTA
(the free Governance Score). No slick pitching — these are CCOs and COOs who
delete anything that smells like a sales blast.

**Primary CTA (everywhere):** the free Governance Score —
`https://centience.ai/assessments/governance-score`
It's the lowest-friction, highest-value entry point: a 0–100 readiness score,
peer benchmark, and priority gaps mapped to the rules, in ~5 minutes.

## 2. Guardrails (read before sending)

- **Connection-request notes: keep ≤ 200 characters** (LinkedIn's free limit).
  All notes below are written to fit. Longer follow-ups are fine *after* they
  accept.
- **Send volume:** stay under ~20–25 connection requests/day per sender account
  to avoid LinkedIn throttling/restriction. 316 leads ≈ 3 weeks of sending.
- **Personalize the first line, always.** Reference their firm, title, or city.
  Generic = ignored (or reported).
- **One CTA per message. Never pitch in the connection note.** Earn the reply
  first.
- **No compliance fear-mongering or fake urgency.** This audience punishes it.
  Lead with a specific, true observation about exams.
- **Match their cadence.** If they reply, drop the sequence and talk like a human.
- **Mobile-first:** short paragraphs, no walls of text, no attachments in early
  touches.

## 3. The cadence (6 touches over ~3 weeks)

| # | Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Connection request + note | Get connected. No pitch. |
| 2 | +1 (after accept) | DM | Thanks + one real insight + soft Governance Score offer |
| 3 | +4 | DM | A specific exam angle relevant to their role; light nudge |
| 4 | +8 | DM | Proof / relevance (regional or peer); reshare the Score link |
| 5 | +13 | DM | Direct, low-pressure ask: run the Score or a 15-min review |
| 6 | +20 | DM | Graceful break-up; leave the door open |

If a contact doesn't accept the connection within ~7 days, send Touch 2 as a
free InMail-style message only if you have Sales Navigator; otherwise let it sit
and retry the connection in 30 days.

## 6. Reply handling (drop the sequence, respond like a human)

- **"Interested / send it"** → Send the Score link + offer a 15-min walkthrough of results. Log in HubSpot as an engaged lead.
- **"We already have this covered / have an MSP"** →
  > Totally fair — most firms do have *something*. Centience is the governance layer *over* your existing IT/MSP: it proves the controls are operating and keeps the evidence standing. The Score is a quick way to see if there are blind spots between you and the provider. Worth 5 min?
- **"Not the right person"** →
  > Appreciate it, {{First Name}} — who owns exam readiness / technology governance at {{Company}}? Happy to connect with them directly (and I'll keep you out of it).
- **"Not now"** →
  > Understood — I'll follow up after your next exam cycle. Leaving you the Score in the meantime: [link].
- **Pricing question early** → Don't quote in DMs. "Depends on firm size and scope — the Score gives us the picture to scope it properly. Want to run it, then talk?"

## 8. Measurement & CRM

- Log every connection, reply, and Score completion against the contact in
  HubSpot (the site already upserts anyone who runs the Score, so replies +
  assessment completions tie together on the record).
- Track per-step: connection accept rate, reply rate, Score-completion rate,
  meetings booked. Expect (rough B2B benchmarks): 25–40% accept, 8–15% reply,
  a few % to Score/meeting. Iterate Touch 1 and Touch 2 first — they move the
  numbers most.
- A/B the connection note per persona in batches of ~50 before scaling the whole
  list.

## 9. Manual sending (no automation)

We are **not** using LinkedIn automation — it violates LinkedIn's terms and gets
accounts banned. These messages are sent **by hand** by a human sender.

Workflow for the sender:
- Work from the companion tracker workbook (`linkedin-outreach-tracker.xlsx`),
  which pre-sorts every lead into a persona track and pre-fills the Touch 1
  connection note per person — copy, paste, send.
- Use the message copy in §4–§5 for Touches 2–6, matched to each lead's
  **Persona Track** column.
- Log dates in the tracker as you go (Connection Sent → Accepted → Touch 2…6 →
  Replied → Status) so nobody gets double-messaged or dropped mid-sequence.
- Respect the daily cap in §2 (~20–25 new connection requests/day per account),
  even by hand — LinkedIn throttles high-volume manual activity too.
- The lead list contains personal contact data; keep it to the sending workbook
  and CRM. It is intentionally **not** committed to the repo.
