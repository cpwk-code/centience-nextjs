# Base Referral & Expansion Kit

Turn each current customer into (1) **two warm intros** to similar firms, and (2) a bigger account — by educating them on services they don't know you offer. One motion, two outcomes.

**Why this works on premium accounts:** they already trust you and pay a premium. A genuine, specific ask lands — and most of them use you for only part of what you do, so "here's what else we handle" surfaces real expansion (e.g., an MSP-only client who doesn't know you now do governance + AI DLP).

**Best channel:** a QBR / relationship call converts far better than a cold email for these accounts. Use the email as the follow-up or for accounts you won't see soon. Ask the happiest, most-established clients first, and tie the ask to a recent win or a renewal.

> **Brand note:** written Centience-branded. If a client still knows you as Compuwork, adjust the opening relationship line accordingly ("you know us as your IT/technology partner").

## Asset 1 — The referral ask (email)

**Subject:** A quick ask — and something you may not know we do

> Hi {First},
>
> Working with {Company} is one of the relationships I value most — thank you for the trust you've placed in us.
>
> I have a quick, two-part ask:
>
> **1. An introduction.** We're growing deliberately — we'd rather have a handful of great clients than a flood. If **one or two firms like yours** come to mind — regulated financial, healthcare, or life-sciences firms, roughly 40–100 people, that want to do technology and compliance right — I'd be grateful for an intro. I've attached a short blurb you can simply forward, so it takes you 30 seconds.
>
> **2. A heads-up on what else we do.** Most clients use us for only part of what we offer. I've attached a one-pager — a few things are newer and clients are often surprised we handle them in-house now: **continuous AI & technology governance, AI data-loss protection, and exam readiness.** If any of it would help {Company}, just reply and I'll walk you through it — no pressure either way.
>
> Grateful for the partnership either way.
>
> Orville Matias
> Founder & CEO, Centience
> (877) 945-7177 · hello@centience.ai

## Asset 3 — QBR / live-conversation talk track (higher conversion)

Three beats, at the end of a good review call:

1. **Appreciation + the frame.** "Before we wrap — {Company}'s exactly the kind of client we want more of, and we're growing deliberately, not by volume. Who are one or two firms like yours I should know?" *(Silence. Let them think of names.)*
2. **Make it concrete.** If they blank: "Think regulated firms your size — finance, healthcare, life sciences — where the CCO or COO is carrying too much. Even one intro is a huge help." Offer to send the forwardable blurb.
3. **The education pivot.** "One more thing — most clients don't realize everything we do now. We've built out continuous AI and technology governance, AI data-loss protection, [and for you specifically: X]. I'll send a quick one-pager; if any of it's useful for {Company}, we'll talk." *(This plants the expansion seed without pitching.)*

## Asset 4 — Optional thank-you (pick one, keep it classy)

Premium relationships often don't need an incentive — but a genuine thank-you helps:
- **Account/service credit** per *closed* referral (e.g., one month credited, or a fixed $ credit). Aligned, not tacky. **Recommended.**
- **Donation** to their charity of choice in their name.
- **Premium gift / dinner** on a closed deal.
- Avoid cash "bounties" — they cheapen a premium relationship.

State it simply: "If an intro becomes a client, we'll credit your account [X] as a thank-you — but the intro's appreciated regardless."

---

## Who to ask, and when

- **Start with your strongest, happiest, most-established accounts.** They give the best intros and the warmest ones.
- **Tie the ask to a moment:** a QBR, a completed project, a clean exam, a renewal.
- **Ask for two *specific* intros, not "anyone."** Naming the ICP (regulated, 40–100 people, finance/healthcare/life-sciences) gets real names; "know anyone?" gets nothing.
- **Sequence by vertical** so referrals compound into case studies: finance clients → finance intros, healthcare → healthcare, etc.

## Tracking (HubSpot)

- Tag the referring customer as the **source** on any intro'd contact.
- A simple **Referral pipeline**: Asked → Intro received → Meeting booked → Won → Thank-you sent.
- Log which service each customer expressed interest in from the one-pager — that's your expansion pipeline.

## The target: 2 intros × your base

Every current customer × 2 intros = a warm pipeline of lookalike premium accounts — the exact profile that gets you the ~15–17 more $150K+ logos to $4M. Ten happy clients asked well ≈ 20 warm, qualified conversations at zero acquisition cost.
