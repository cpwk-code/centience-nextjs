## The problem you already know

Your client's investors hire an assessor. The assessor sends you a questionnaire. You answer it, they come back with findings, you write management responses, and eventually the report goes out.

Then your next client's investors hire the same assessor, and it starts again — same questions, same evidence, assembled from scratch.

Nothing you produced the first time is reusable, because none of it was ever a record. It was a document you wrote once and filed somewhere.

Meanwhile the findings are real. Access reviews with no log. Retention configured but never tested. A policy that commits to something the environment doesn't enforce. You can fix all of it and still fail the next assessment, because **fixing a control and being able to prove it operated are different things.**

---

## What Centience does

We operate the technology controls underneath your governance programme, and we keep the evidence as it is produced.

- **Control testing against your live environment** — access, multi-factor enforcement, retention configuration, conditional access, backup verification. Results timestamped, not screenshotted.
- **Findings with an owner and a citation** — every finding names the obligation or the policy clause it comes from, and who is accountable for it. Not our opinion; the rule.
- **An evidence record that accumulates** — when a control is verified, the observed configuration is captured. When it drifts, you know.
- **Policy and WSP generation** from what the engine actually finds, so the document and the environment agree.
- **Exception governance** — a risk you accept deliberately, with a named owner, a rationale and a review date, reads as governance. A red light with no decision record reads as neglect. Same facts.
- **Incident workflow** including the Regulation S-P notification clock, so a breach response is timed and documented rather than improvised.

The result is that the next questionnaire is a retrieval exercise instead of a project — and when someone asks whether a finding was cured, you attach the record instead of asserting it.

## What we don't claim

This matters more to you than a feature list, so it's on the page.

**We are not your compliance officer.** Regulatory interpretation, your WSP ownership and your examination strategy stay with you or your compliance counsel. We translate obligations into technical controls and evidence.

**We do not assess our own work.** Centience verifies and evidences the controls it operates. Where genuine independence is required, an independent assessor consumes our evidence — they don't compete with it. We will not tell you we are both the builder and the auditor.

**We are not an MSP.** Your existing IT or provider stays in place. We govern the technology regardless of who operates it, and where we're authorised to enforce a specific control domain, that authority is named in writing and reversible.
