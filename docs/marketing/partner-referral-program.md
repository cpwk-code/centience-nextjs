**Owner:** Orville Matias · **Built with:** Claude · **Date:** August 2026
**Scope:** **Financial services only** (RIAs, broker-dealers, hedge/private funds). Healthcare and life-science partners are deliberately out of scope for now — see §9.
**Companion docs:** `path-to-4m-h2-2026-gtm-execution.md` (§6 — "channel is the highest-ROI new source") · `base-referral-and-expansion-kit.md` (customer referrals — *this* doc is third-party partners) · `linkedin-outreach-sequence.md`

> **The thesis.** Our buyer (CCO / CISO / COO at a regulated firm) buys on trust and almost never from cold outbound. But a handful of people **already sit in that trust seat** — the firm's compliance consultant, its E&O/cyber broker, its securities counsel, its M&A advisor, its fund administrator. Each is asked "who should handle our IT and cyber?" several times a year and today has no good answer. **One good partner is worth more than an entire cold list.** Target: 8–12 productive partners, each sending 2–4 qualified firms a year.
>
> **Why they say yes (this is the whole pitch):** we do the thing they *can't* do and don't want to own — run and technically enforce the IT/cyber stack — and we make their work look good, because their WSP finally has evidence behind it. **Complementary, not competitive**, to every partner in this document. Lead with that, not with the commission.

## 1. Commission architecture

Referral fees here cluster at **5–20% of first-year ACV** for services of our type ([Refgrow benchmarks](https://refgrow.com/referral-fees)). Our ACVs are large enough that points are real money, so keep it simple — three tiers, one page.

| Tier | Partner does | Commission | On a $65K new-logo ACV |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Refer** | Warm intro, then steps back | **10% of Year-1 contract value**, on cash collected | **$6,500** |
| **Co-sell** | Intro + joins the Governance Review + vouches | **15% Year-1, then 5% Years 2–3** | **$9,750 + ~$6,500 over 2 yrs** |
| **Embedded** | Resells us inside their own engagement, owns the client relationship | **20–25% margin** on platform + cyber lines | negotiated |

**Rules that keep it clean:**
- Paid on **collected revenue**, never on signature.
- **90-day registration window** per intro'd firm; renews while the deal is actively progressing in HubSpot.
- Existing Centience/Compuwork clients and anyone already in an open pipeline stage are **excluded** — publish this so nobody feels cheated later.
- Clawback if the client churns inside 6 months.
- **One page.** A 12-page channel agreement kills a boutique-consultant partnership before it starts.

**The non-cash track — you will need it.** Several of the best partners are ethically or contractually blocked from taking a commission (§8). Offer the same value in a form they *can* accept:
- **Reciprocal referrals** — we send them compliance/legal/insurance work. Usually worth more to a professional-services firm than a fee.
- **Co-branded Governance Score** — they deliver our assessment to their book as their own diagnostic (their brand, our engine). Enormous pull for a consultant.
- **Partner pricing** — they buy at partner rates and bill their client at their own.
- **Charitable donation** in their name, or **CE-eligible co-hosted content** — the standard answer for attorneys and CPAs.

## 8. Legal & regulatory guardrails ⚠️ read before paying anyone

Referral fees are ordinary and legal here, but three partner types carry real constraints. **Have counsel paper the template agreement once**, then reuse it.

| Situation | Constraint | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Paying an attorney** (Tier C) | Bar rules broadly bar giving anything of value for a client recommendation and restrict fee-sharing with non-lawyers | **Never offer cash.** Reciprocal referrals + co-authored content only |
| **Paying a CPA/audit firm** | AICPA Rule 503: commission **absolutely prohibited** if they perform audit/review/compilation for that client — disclosure does not cure it; permitted for other clients **with written disclosure** ([ref](https://www.njcpa.org/stayinformed/hubs/topics/commissions-and-contingent-fees)) | Non-cash by default; let their ethics counsel opt in |
| **Paying an individual registered rep** | [FINRA Rule 3270](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/3270): outside compensation from a non-firm source is an outside business activity requiring **written notice to and review by their BD** | Contract with the **firm**, not the individual; require written confirmation of home-office approval |
| **Paying an RIA or its principal** | Not the SEC Marketing Rule (206(4)-1 governs soliciting *advisory clients*, not vendor referrals) — but a vendor fee is a **conflict of interest** the adviser may need to disclose | Put disclosure obligations on the partner in the agreement |
| **A compliance consultant recommending us to their client** (Tier A) | Independence/objectivity optics — the exact thing their client trusts them for | Require **disclosure of the fee to the referred firm. Non-negotiable.** It protects the partner, and a partner who feels exposed stops referring |
| **All partners** | — | Written agreement; no authority to speak for Centience; no client-data access; no guarantees or "compliant"/"certified" claims on our behalf (same discipline as §8 of the GTM plan) |

**Bottom line:** default to **fee + mandatory disclosure** for consultants, brokers, platforms, M&A advisors and fund admins; **non-cash reciprocity** for attorneys and attest-side CPAs.

## 9. Deliberately excluded

- **IT MSPs / MSSPs / regional IT firms.** Cut per direction — not pursuing peer-MSP referrals or white-label resale.
- **Vendor co-sell** (Microsoft, Strac, Keeper, Dropsuite). Not a referral-partner motion and IT-channel rather than finance-channel; excluded here. Still free pipeline if you want it later — separate doc.
- **Healthcare & life sciences.** Finance first. Also a product-honesty issue: HIPAA/CCPA/GDPR are *mapped* in the platform but have **no vertical-specific enforcement checks** (all 13 checks are FINRA/SEC-oriented). Don't build that channel ahead of the product.
- **Compliance-software vendors** — COMPLY (ComplySci / RIA in a Box), Smarsh, Global Relay, Archer. **Coopetition:** they do communications surveillance, we do infrastructure enforcement. An integration thesis may make sense someday; a referral relationship does not.

**Not commission channels, but worth budget** because they create the credibility partners want before they refer: [**NSCP**](https://www.nscp.org/) (national conference + [vendor directory](https://www.nscp.org/vendor-directory) — the highest-density room of our exact buyer), **IAA**, **FSI**, **SIFMA**, **Hedge Fund Association**; **custodian vendor/provider programs** (Schwab Advisor Services, Fidelity Integration Xchange, BNY Pershing, Altruist) — get listed, then work the field-consultant layer; and **advisor-tech analysts** (Kitces, T3, F2 Strategy) who get asked "who should we use?" constantly.

## 10. The partner pitch (use this, not a commission-led email)

**Subject:** The IT side of your clients' compliance programs

> Hi {First},
>
> You write the policies for firms like {Client Type} — and then someone has to actually make the technology match what the WSP says. That's the part we own.
>
> Centience runs and continuously enforces the IT and cybersecurity stack for regulated firms: MFA, encryption, retention, email/data-loss controls, incident response — with **evidence** an examiner will accept, checked continuously rather than once a year. We're not a compliance consultant and we're not software you hand a client; we're the operator behind the controls, and we make your program provably real.
>
> Two ways this usually works with firms like yours:
> 1. We become your standing remediation partner — when a client's technology can't back up the policy, you have somewhere to send them.
> 2. We run our **Governance Score** on a couple of your clients, co-branded to you, so you can show them exactly where the exposure sits.
>
> We pay a referral fee on anything that closes and we disclose it to the client — your objectivity matters more to us than the deal. Worth 20 minutes?
>
> Orville Matias · Founder & CEO, Centience · (877) 945-7177 · hello@centience.ai

**Variants:** for **brokers** (Tier B) lead with "every applicant who fails your cyber questionnaire is a firm we can fix"; for **M&A** (Tier E) lead with "your diligence finds the IT gap — we're who closes it before the deadline"; for **fund admins** (Tier F) lead with the investor-ODD questionnaire.

**Then:** offer to run a **free Governance Score on one of their clients** (with permission). That artifact — a real gap report on a firm they know — converts a polite partner into an active one. Same wedge as the base-expansion motion, aimed one layer out.
