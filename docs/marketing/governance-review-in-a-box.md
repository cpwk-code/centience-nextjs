# Governance Review in a Box — Base Expansion Sales Kit

**Purpose:** The copy-paste, repeatable kit to run the base-expansion motion in `path-to-4m-h2-2026-gtm-execution.md`. This is what **you** use for the first ~10 reviews and what the **first hire** runs after. The Score does the diagnosis; this kit does the conversation.

> **Defaults used below are tunable** — confirm the four inputs at the end of the H2 plan (whale count, current attach, bundle price, capacity) and we lock the numbers. Pricing here is a placeholder 3-tier table.

---

## 0. Prep per account (10 min, before any outreach)

From the Governance Score (run on all 75 — you own the data, no onboarding):
- **Score** (0–100) + the **2–3 gaps** that map to a real exam finding (Reg S-P, 17a-4/4511, 3110).
- **Peer benchmark** ("firms your size average 74; you're at 61").
- **One dollar-or-exam consequence** per gap (the urgency line).
- **Track:** Whale → Managed · Core → Platform · Tail → hold/light.

## 2. The Governance Readiness Review (30–45 min call script)

**Goal:** move Score → Proposal. Structure = *Frame → Reveal → Consequence → Vision → Offer → Next step.*

**(2 min) Frame.** "This isn't a sales call — it's the exam-readiness snapshot on the environment we manage for you. I'll show you where you stand, where the gaps are, and options. No pressure to decide today."

**(3 min) Discovery (confirm stakes).**
- "When's your next exam, or when was your last?"
- "What did the examiner focus on last time?"
- "Who owns compliance day-to-day — and how do they prove a control was active *between* exams?"
- "Any AI tools in use — Copilot, ChatGPT? Who approved them?"

**(8 min) Reveal (the Score).** Walk the 0–100, the per-domain breakdown, the benchmark. Land on the 2–3 real gaps. Plain English, not NIST: *"You can't currently prove MFA was enforced continuously for the last 6 months — an examiner will ask for exactly that."*

**(5 min) Consequence.** For each gap, the real-world cost: deficiency letter, Reg S-P 30-day breach clock exposure, 17a-4 records finding, un-inventoried shadow AI. Make it their exam, not a framework.

**(5 min) Vision — "continuous + evidenced."** "The difference between a checklist and what we do: controls monitored continuously, evidence assembled automatically, and when the examiner asks for 6 months of proof, it's a download — not a scramble. In your CCO's language, not an engineer's."

**(5 min) Offer (route by track).**
- **Core → Platform:** "We add the Governance Platform onto what we already run for you — continuous monitoring, evidence vault, WSP, AI/vendor registers, quarterly re-score. Bundles onto your existing invoice: [price]."
- **Whale → Managed:** "We operate the whole governance program — infra + cyber + AI/data — and maintain your exam evidence continuously. Fully managed: [price]."

**(2 min) Next step.** "I'll send a one-page proposal today. If it looks right, we can have you live and generating evidence in two weeks." → book the follow-up before hanging up.

**Discipline:** say **"exam-ready / evidenced / guided remediation,"** never "compliant/enforced" unless it's the Managed tier you operate.

## 5. Objection library (starter — grows from your recorded reviews)

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already have a compliance consultant." | "Great — they tell you *what's* required. We *operate and evidence* it continuously, so when they or an examiner asks for proof, it's already assembled. We make your consultant's job easier." |
| "We do an annual assessment." | "That covers one day a year. An examiner wants proof the control was active every day *between* assessments. That gap is exactly where deficiencies come from." |
| "Too expensive / not budgeted." | "It's bundled onto what you already pay us — one vendor, one invoice. Compare it to one deficiency letter or a client leaving over a data incident." |
| "We're too small to be examined." | "Reg S-P's small-firm deadline was June 2026 — it already applies to you. Size doesn't exempt you; it just means fewer resources to scramble." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Fair. The snapshot's the useful part regardless — want me to send the 3 gaps in writing so your team can at least close #1 this month? [keeps momentum + re-engagement hook]" |
| "Is this just a dashboard?" (the Vanta compare) | "Dashboards show you a framework. We translate to *your* regulator's language, and we can actually operate and fix it — not just flag it. That's the difference." |

## 7. How this becomes not-just-you (the replication loop)

1. Run your first ~10 reviews from this kit. **Record every one (Granola).**
2. After each, drop the new objections + winning lines back into §5 and the talk track in §2.
3. Once §2 and §5 are stable (~10 reviews), the kit is a **training doc** — a Governance Account Manager runs Core reviews from it; you keep the whales.
4. The app generates the artifacts (Score PDF, WSP, gap report, proposal draft), so a junior AM delivers senior-quality output.

> **Automation note:** wiring outreach → HubSpot → Resend nurture, and pulling Apollo lists, requires authorizing those connectors in an interactive session first. This kit runs manually today; automate once the motion is proven.
