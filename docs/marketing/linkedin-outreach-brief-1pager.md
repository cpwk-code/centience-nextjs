### Daily rhythm
- **Max ~20–25 new connection requests/day.** Even by hand, higher trips LinkedIn's limits and risks the account. 316 leads ≈ 3 weeks.
- Work top-to-bottom through the tracker. Log every action the day you do it.

| # | When | What |

---

### Using the tracker (3 steps)
1. Copy the **Touch 1 note** (column J) → open their **LinkedIn URL** (column G) → send the connection request with the note. Put the date in **Connection Sent**.
2. When they accept, mark **Accepted?** and start the follow-ups on the schedule above, logging each Touch date.
3. Update **Status** (Not started / In sequence / Replied / Meeting / Not interested) so nothing is double-sent or dropped.

---

### Golden rules
- **Never pitch in the connection note.** Get connected first.
- **Personalize the first line** if you tweak anything — reference their firm or city.
- **No fear-mongering or fake urgency.** This audience deletes it. Lead with a real, specific observation about exams.
- **One CTA per message** — always the Governance Score link.
- **Keep the connection note ≤ 200 characters** (the pre-filled ones already are).
